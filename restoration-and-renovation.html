Restoration and Renovation

A considered path from property damage and cleanup to repairs, reconstruction and renewed spaces.

Our services

Commercial ConstructionIndustrial ConstructionInstitutional and CivilInfrastructureRestoration and Renovation

Restore what matters. Build what’s next.

When a property needs recovery or renewal, the next steps should be clear. We bring cleanup, specialist remediation coordination, repairs and rebuilding into a practical plan that reflects the condition of the property and your priorities.

Water and flood damage

Water removal, drying coordination and repairs to affected areas, with attention to the source of moisture and the rebuilding scope.

Fire and smoke damage

Cleanup and repair planning for fire-affected spaces, including smoke residue, damaged materials and coordinated reconstruction.

Wind and storm damage

Property stabilization and repair coordination for weather-damaged roofs, exteriors and interiors.

Mould remediation

Assessment and coordination of contained remediation work, with specialist providers and attention to the underlying moisture problem.

Reconstruction

Rebuild damaged spaces through coordinated demolition, structural repairs, trade work and interior finishes.

Asbestos abatement coordination

Coordinate assessment and removal with qualified specialist providers before renovation or reconstruction work proceeds.

Biohazard cleanup coordination

Arrange specialist cleanup for affected areas, with a defined scope, controlled access and careful handling of the property.

Contents cleaning

Cleaning and recovery coordination for affected belongings, furnishings and equipment, based on their condition.

Deep cleaning

Detailed cleanup of affected spaces and hard-to-reach surfaces as part of a restoration or renovation plan.

Renovation and general contracting

Update layouts, finishes and building features through one coordinated construction scope, from planning to completion.

Commercial property recovery

Plan restoration work around business access, priority areas and the practical steps needed to return spaces to use.

Disaster cleanup

Coordinate debris removal, property cleanup and the transition from initial recovery work to longer-term repairs.

A practical plan.
A coordinated team.

We establish what needs attention, what requires a specialist and how the work should be sequenced. The aim is a clear recovery plan with a defined scope and a practical transition into reconstruction or renovation.
