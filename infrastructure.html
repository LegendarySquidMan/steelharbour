Infrastructure

Underground services, sewer and watermain work, and rehabilitation that support stronger communities.

Our services

Commercial ConstructionIndustrial ConstructionInstitutional and CivilInfrastructureRestoration and Renovation

The foundations of everyday life.

Much of the infrastructure a community relies on is out of sight. Our focus is on the practical field work and coordination that help maintain, improve and renew those essential systems.

Sewer and watermain

Construction and renewal coordination for storm, sanitary and watermain systems, including connections and related site work.

Hydrovac and excavation

Coordinate vacuum excavation, underground utility exposure and conventional excavation to suit the site and project requirements.

Inspection and cleaning

Support sewer cleaning, jetting and CCTV inspection scopes that help establish condition and inform maintenance decisions.

Trenchless rehabilitation

Coordinate specialist pipeline renewal scopes, including cured-in-place pipe lining where appropriate to the system and condition.

Catch basins and manholes

Installation, repair and rehabilitation scopes for drainage structures and access points within the broader network.

Utility and site reinstatement

Bring utility interfaces, backfilling, grading and concrete or asphalt reinstatement into the construction sequence.

A practical plan.
A coordinated team.

We begin with the system, the site conditions and the limits of the work area. Coordination with utility owners, project teams and specialist providers helps create a clear path from inspection to reinstatement.
